Amplified
Intrinsic Arc Subclass Buff
Obtained by reaching 100% Counter Progress through Arc Kills within 6 seconds of each, or through Aspects.
Rank-And-File = 25% | Elite = 50% | Miniboss+ = 100% | Guardians = 50%

Amplified:
+50 Mobility, increases Slide Distance to 8.5 meters, +40 Handling, and 0.95x Handling Duration Multiplier for 15 seconds.
Combatants are less accurate at targeting the wearer and grants 15% Damage Resist against Combatants while active.
While Amplified, sprinting for 2.5 seconds grants Speed Booster.

Speed Booster:
Maximizes Movement Speed, increased Slide Distance to 11 meters, 25% Increased Jump Height, and 15% Damage Resist against Combatants.
Speed Booster lingers for 2 seconds until no longer sprinting, even after Amplified runs out.
Performing a Speed Booster Slide permanently increases base Slide Distance from 6.5 meters to 8.5 meters until death.

Bolt Charge
Upon scoring a Magazine-Based amount of Weapon Damage Instances within 5 seconds of each while Bolt Charge is active:
The next damage instance from any source will grant a stack of Bolt Charge.
Swapping weapons resets the counter. Can only gain up to 1 stack per simultaneous damage instance.

Damage instances needed to gain a stack:
(15% of Magazine, rounded down) + 1
Swords and GLs: 2
3-Ammo Snipers: 1

Gaining a Bolt Charge stack grants 2.5% Melee Ability Energy per Bolt Charge stack obtained, generally granting 25% Melee Ability Energy over the 10 stacks needed to discharge.
Acquiring over x10 stacks through abilities will still grant the corresponding Ability Gains (For example, being at x9 and acquiring x4 will still grant 10% Melee Energy)

After 0.5 seconds of scoring an Ability Hit or dealing Unpowered Melee Damage while at x10 Bolt Charge stacks:
Discharges an Arc Bolt over the damaged enemy, dealing up to 405 Bolt Splash + 270 Ground Splash = 675 Total [36 Bolt Splash + 30 Ground Splash] Arc Damage over ? meters.
Does not count as Ability Damage, only as Generic Arc Damage.

Triggers Discharge:
Volatile Explosions | Freeze Shatter (Not auto-shatter on Bosses) | Tangle Explosions | Threadlings | Unraveling Threads | Suspend Snap on Bosses | Forerunner's The Rock

Do NOT trigger Discharge:
Jolt's Chain Lightning | Scorch and Ignitions (from Non-Abilities) | Crystal Shatter (from Non-Abilities) | Gifted Conviction's Explosives
